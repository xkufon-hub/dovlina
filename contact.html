Questions, observations and thoughtful suggestions are welcome. Please include the page title when writing about something on this website.

Start with what caught your eye

A small detail can open a larger conversation. You might have noticed a pattern in a photograph, a connection between two articles or a sentence that could be clearer. A brief note explaining what you saw is a useful place to begin.

Questions and corrections

When writing about a particular page, include its title and the passage or image you have in mind. This gives your message context and makes it easier to understand. General ideas for future subjects are welcome as well.

Keep exploring

The collection is intended to be read at your own pace. Move from a wide view to a closer detail, compare shapes and textures, and follow the connections that interest you. There is no required order and no single interpretation to arrive at.
